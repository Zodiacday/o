# PreviewPort brand

## Identity

- Product name: **PreviewPort**
- Descriptor: **Live Flutter previews on your iPhone**
- Primary tagline: **Your app. On your phone. In seconds.**
- Positioning: A QR-powered live preview companion for Flutter web apps.
- App Store category: Developer Tools

## Product family

- Mobile app: `PreviewPort`
- Developer CLI: `PreviewPort CLI`
- CLI command: `previewport`
- Connection scheme: `previewport://connect`
- Legacy scheme accepted by the app: `fluttergo://connect`

## App Store copy

- Name: `PreviewPort`
- Subtitle: `Flutter previews on iPhone`
- Opening description: `PreviewPort is a QR-powered live preview companion for Flutter web apps.`

## Visual system

- Pitch Black OLED: `#000000` (zero-glow pure black backdrop)
- Pure Surface: `#000000` (machined panels with hairline cyan borders)
- Electric cyan: `#00E5FF`
- Sky blue: `#38BDF8`
- Cool steel: `#94A3B8`
- Primary text: `#F8FAFC`

The visual mark is a compact portal ring with a live-status dot. The animated
orb can be used as an in-app motion or empty-state element, but the launcher
mark should remain simple enough to read at small iPhone icon sizes.

The first transparent logo asset is stored at
`assets/previewport-logo-transparent.png`.

## Voice

Clear, concise, technical, and reassuring. Prefer “Scan to Preview” and
“Preview Ready” over hype or claims about magic. Mention Flutter descriptively
in product copy; do not use the Flutter logo or make Flutter part of the
PreviewPort product name.
